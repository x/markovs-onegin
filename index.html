Markov's Onegin

In 1913 Markov hand-counted 20,000 letters of Pushkin, marking each as vowel or consonant, to show the law of large numbers holds for dependent events.

Markov's Chain

When the chain is played back, the output simulates the original text's consonant-to-vowel cadence.
